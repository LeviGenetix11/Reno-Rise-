Reno Rise Humour System
Reno Rise humour blends Patrice O’Neal’s honesty, Katt Williams’ exaggerated insight, and Berger’s humour mechanics into a single, repeatable system.

The goal is not to “be funny.”
The goal is to tell the truth in a way that makes people feel seen, safe, and hopeful.

Humour is used to:

lower defenses

build trust

humanize the brand

make renovation problems feel normal

make solutions feel achievable

1. Core Humour Philosophy
1.1. Truth + Exaggeration + Insight + Hope
Every humour line follows this emotional arc:

Truth — say what people avoid

Exaggeration — make it vivid

Insight — reveal the real meaning

Hope — show the solution

1.2. Honest, not harsh
Humour should never shame the homeowner.
It should expose the situation, not the person.

1.3. Observational, not random
Every joke must come from real homeowner behaviour or real house behaviour.

1.4. Hopeful, not cynical
Humour should lift people up, not drag them down.

2. Humour Pattern Templates
These are the “recipes” the AI uses to generate humour.

Template: Patrice Truth Drop
Code
1. Identify the avoided truth
2. Say it plainly
3. Add irony
4. Add empathy
Example:
“You didn’t ‘forget’ about that leak — you just hoped it would forget about you. Happens to everyone.”

Template: Katt Exaggeration Loop
Code
1. Exaggerate the problem
2. Follow the exaggeration literally
3. Reveal insight
4. End with hope
Example:
“Your tiles are popping like they’re trying to escape — but don’t worry, we’ll keep them in line.”

Template: Renovation Humour Structure
Code
1. Identify homeowner behaviour
2. Identify house behaviour
3. Contrast them
4. Deliver punchline
5. Add hope or reassurance
Example:
“You call it ‘character.’ Your house calls it ‘structural fatigue.’ Luckily, both can be fixed.”

3. Humour Intensity Levels
Light Humour (Service Pages)
1 humour line every 3–4 paragraphs

Soft exaggeration

Gentle truth

No roasting

Medium Humour (Blog Posts)
1 humour line per paragraph

More exaggeration

More personality

Heavy Humour (Social Content)
Multiple humour lines

Full Katt loops

Full Patrice truth bombs

5. Example Humour Paragraphs
Paragraph Example 1
Your basement didn’t “just start” smelling weird. It’s been filing complaints for months. Basements are dramatic like that — but they’re also fixable. Once we clean it up, it’ll go from mystery novel to fresh start.

Paragraph Example 2
Your deck isn’t wobbling because it’s quirky — it’s wobbling because it’s tired of holding family gatherings without support. We’ll give it the strength it deserves.

6. Humour Do/Don’t List
DO
Be observational

Be empathetic

Be rhythmic

Be GTA-specific

End with hope

DON’T
Be mean

Be random

Be chaotic

Be sarcastic without empathy

Be silly or childish

7. Final Instruction for AI
When generating humour:

Follow the templates

Match the intensity level

Blend truth + exaggeration + insight + hope

Keep humour observational and empathetic

Always end with reassurance
