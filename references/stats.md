National Renovation Market Statistics (Canada)
1.1. Market Size
Canada’s renovation, maintenance, and repair sector is worth $107B+ per year
This sector is larger than new home construction in total economic value
1.2. Employment
Renovation and repair support 525,000+ jobs nationwide
57% of all residential construction jobs come from renovation, not new builds
1.3. Homeowner Demand
49% of Canadians were renovating or planning to renovate in 2024
Average renovation budget: $19,000 (up from $10,000 in 2019)
1.4. ROI
Kitchens & bathrooms return 75–100% of their cost — highest ROI projects in Canada
1.5. Underground Cash Economy
Renovation work accounts for 32.7% of Canada’s underground cash economy — the largest share of any industry
Renovation Market Trends (2025–2026)
2.1. Renovation Spending Growth
Renovation investment rose 19% from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026 (from $19.52B to $23.28B)
New construction investment declined 1.5% in the same period
2.2. Single‑Dwelling Renovation Surge
Renovation spending on single homes increased 23.4% year‑over‑year (from $12.32B to $15.20B)
Renovation spending is now 2.5× higher than new single‑dwelling construction
2.3. Regional Shifts
Ontario shifted toward renovation spending as new construction declined
Québec saw a 62% increase in renovation investment year‑over‑year (from $4.81B to $7.79B)
