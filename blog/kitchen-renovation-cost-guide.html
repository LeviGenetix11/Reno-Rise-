Your kitchen isn't outdated because you neglected it. Kitchens don't announce that they've fallen behind — they just get quieter about it, one sticking drawer and one flickering light at a time, until a cabinet door comes off in your hand and you finally admit it's time.

That's the number above. Here's what actually moves it — and how to make sure the quote you get reflects your kitchen, not a national average.

What Drives Kitchen Renovation Cost in the GTA

Four things separate a $16,000 kitchen from a $70,000 kitchen: whether the layout is changing, whether plumbing or electrical is moving, the finish level you choose, and who's holding the tools. Moving a sink or a gas line alone can add $2,500-$7,000 before a single finish is picked — not because contractors are padding the bill, but because permits, inspections, and rerouting are real line items, not suggestions.

Size matters less than people expect. A small, efficient kitchen with high-end finishes can cost more than a large kitchen finished modestly. It's not about square footage — it's about how many decisions you're paying someone to execute well. Our kitchen remodeling team walks every GTA home in person before pricing a single decision.

Kitchen Renovation Cost Breakdown by Category

A kitchen renovation is really five smaller projects wearing one budget. Here's what each one runs on its own, and roughly how much of the total it eats.

Photo by Curtis Adams on Pexels

Custom Cabinetry Cost

Cabinetry is almost always the biggest line item, typically 30-35% of the budget. Stock cabinets run $120-$360 per linear foot installed. Semi-custom lands at $360-$850. Custom cabinetry cost climbs to $850-$1,450+ per linear foot once you're choosing wood species, finish, and hardware individually rather than off a shelf.

Countertop Installation Cost

Countertop installation cost depends almost entirely on material. Laminate starts around $35-$55 per square foot installed. Quartz and butcher block sit in the $65-$150 range. Natural stone like granite or marble can reach $130-$260 per square foot for a standard island and perimeter run.

Backsplash Installation Cost

Backsplash installation cost is one of the smaller categories, but the range is wide: basic subway tile runs $15-$35 per square foot installed, while handmade or natural stone tile can hit $55-$95 per square foot once labour and waterproofing are factored in.

Tile Installation Cost (Flooring)

For kitchen flooring, tile installation cost typically falls between $12 and $30 per square foot for ceramic or porcelain, and $22-$42 per square foot for natural stone — including substrate prep, which matters more in older GTA homes with uneven subfloors than almost anywhere else in the renovation.

Kitchen Remodel vs. Kitchen Renovation: Does the Word Change the Price?

Not directly, but the distinction still matters. A remodel changes the layout, footprint, or function of the space. A renovation restores or updates what's already there without moving walls or plumbing. Renovations are cheaper because they skip permits and structural work; remodels cost more because you're paying for the freedom to change your mind about where the sink goes.

The GTA-Specific Cost No Calculator Warns You About: A Bad Contractor

Here's the part most cost guides skip. According to the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation, Ontario's renovation, maintenance, and repair sector is worth over $107 billion a year, and renovation spending is now growing roughly 2.5 times faster than new construction. That demand is good news for homeowners — until you notice that nearly a third of all renovation work in Canada happens off the books, cash-only, uninsured.

That's not a rounding error. It's the reason two "kitchen renovation" quotes for the same job can be $15,000 apart — one of them just isn't planning to pull a permit, carry insurance, or be around if something fails. A cheap quote from an unlicensed contractor isn't a discount. It's a cost you haven't been charged yet.

Before you sign anything, confirm the contractor is licensed where required, carries liability insurance of at least $2M, offers a written contract and warranty, and can show a real portfolio with more than a handful of reviews. The Canadian Home Builders' Association publishes renovator standards worth checking before you sign anything. "I can start tomorrow" sounds like good news. It usually means they don't have a pipeline of other clients — and you should ask why. We renovate homes across the entire Greater Toronto Area, and every crew we send out carries the paperwork above.

Why a Kitchen Renovation Cost Calculator Gets It Wrong

Photo by Karola G on Pexels

A kitchen renovation cost calculator is fine for a ballpark — most tools just multiply your square footage by a per-square-foot average and adjust for finish level. What they can't see is your home. They don't know your electrical panel is original to 1987, that your subfloor dips half an inch near the window, or that the wall you want gone is load-bearing.

Those are exactly the variables that swing a real quote by $5,000 or more in either direction. A calculator gives you a number. A free in-home assessment gives you a number based on your actual kitchen — which is the only number that matters once work starts.

Is a Kitchen Renovation Worth It?

Photo by Peter Vang on Pexels

Kitchens and bathrooms return roughly 75-100% of their cost at resale — the highest return of any renovation category. That's not a promise that every dollar comes back; it's a range, and where you land in it depends on whether your finishes match your neighbourhood or outclass it. A $90,000 kitchen in a $500,000 house doesn't return 90%. The same kitchen in a $1.4M house often does.

How to Lower Kitchen Renovation Cost Without Cutting Corners

  • Keep the existing footprint — moving plumbing or gas lines is the single biggest budget multiplier.
  • Reface cabinets instead of replacing them if the boxes are still square and solid.
  • Choose quartz over natural stone for a similar look with lower maintenance and cost.
  • Get itemized quotes so cabinetry, countertop installation cost, and tile installation cost are broken out separately — vague line items hide markup.
  • Set aside 10-15% of your budget as a contingency. Older homes tell the truth about their wiring and subfloors once demo starts, not before.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a kitchen renovation cost in the GTA?

Most homeowners spend $22,000-$65,000, with mid-range projects averaging around $38,000. Cost scales with cabinetry quality, countertop material, and whether the layout changes.

What percentage of my home's value should I spend on a kitchen renovation?

Most industry guidance points to 10-15% of your home's current value. A $38,000 kitchen makes sense on a $1.2M GTA home; the same number on a $500,000 home is a harder sell to a future buyer.

Is it cheaper to reface cabinets or replace them?

Refacing typically costs 40-60% less than full replacement — but only if the cabinet boxes are still square and solid. If they're not, refacing just dresses up a problem that resurfaces in a year or two.

How much does custom cabinetry cost compared to stock cabinets?

Stock cabinets run $120-$360 per linear foot installed. Custom cabinetry cost climbs to $850-$1,450+ per linear foot depending on wood species, finish, and hardware.

How long does a kitchen renovation take in the GTA?

A basic refresh takes 2-3 weeks. A mid-range renovation typically runs 6-8 weeks. Anything involving a layout change, permits, or structural work can stretch to 10-14 weeks.

Should I trust an online kitchen renovation cost calculator?

Use it for a rough starting point only. It can't see your electrical panel, your subfloor, or your actual layout — which is exactly what moves a real quote by thousands of dollars. A free in-home assessment catches what a calculator can't.
