Flooring

Flooring, Done Right the First Time

Flooring takes more daily wear than almost anything else in your home, and the wrong material for a room, like solid hardwood in a basement, causes problems no matter how well it's installed.

We help you match material to the room first, then install it on a properly prepped subfloor, which is the step most flooring failures actually trace back to.

What's Included

  • Hardwood, engineered hardwood, and laminate installation
  • Tile and natural stone flooring
  • Luxury vinyl plank and vinyl flooring
  • Subfloor levelling and moisture barrier prep
  • Removal and disposal of old flooring
