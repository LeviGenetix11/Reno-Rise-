Storm Door Installation in the GTA

Home Services Storm Door Installation

Quick answer: storm door installation typically costs $400 to $900 installed. It's a small, honest job, and this page keeps things that way rather than dressing it up.

A storm door does two things: it protects your main entry door from GTA winters, and it adds an insulating air gap that cuts down on the draft an older front entry tends to let through. It's a small addition, and it's worth being straight about when it's actually worth doing.

What the Installation Involves

We measure the existing entry frame, fit the storm door and its frame against it, and seal and adjust the weatherstripping so the door actually closes tight. The closer and hardware get fitted and tested last. Most installs run $400-$900 depending on door quality, aluminum versus full-view glass, and whether the existing frame needs adjustment to fit properly.

When a Storm Door Isn't the Right Fit

If your main entry door itself is old, warped, or already drafty, replacing that door through our door installation service fixes the actual problem better than adding a storm door in front of it. And if your entry door is already a modern, well-sealed unit, a storm door won't save you much on energy costs, at that point it's really about protecting the door and adding curb appeal, which is a fine reason to want one, just not the one most people expect.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does storm door installation cost?

Storm door installation typically costs $400-$900 installed, depending on the door quality (aluminum vs. full-view glass, self-storing options) and whether the existing entry frame needs adjustment to fit it properly.

Does a storm door actually save on energy bills?

Modestly, and mostly if your main entry door is older or already drafty. The insulating air gap helps, but if your entry door is already a modern, well-sealed unit, a storm door adds cost with limited efficiency benefit, its main value at that point is protecting the door and adding curb appeal.

Do I need a permit to install a storm door?

No. Storm door installation is a straightforward exterior fit-and-seal job that doesn't require a permit.
