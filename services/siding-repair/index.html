Siding Repair: What to Know Before You Hire

Siding repair usually gets called in after a storm cracks a panel, or after someone notices a section that's started to warp or pull away from the wall. Left alone, a damaged section of siding stops being cosmetic fast — it's your home's water barrier with a hole in it.

Professionals typically match the existing panel profile and colour as closely as the manufacturer allows, and check the house wrap underneath the damaged section before closing it back up, since siding damage often means water has already gotten behind it.

What This Work Typically Includes

  • Panel matching to existing colour and profile
  • House wrap inspection behind damaged sections
  • Storm and impact damage repair
  • Warping and buckling diagnosis and fix
