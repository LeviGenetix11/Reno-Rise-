Framing and Carpentry

Framing and Carpentry: What to Know Before You Hire

Framing is the part of a renovation nobody sees once it's finished, which is exactly why it's the part you can't afford to have done wrong. A layout change is only as good as the framing behind the drywall.

Professionals typically frame to current code and engineer stamped drawings when structural changes are involved, then bring in the same carpentry standard for custom built-ins and trim work.

What This Work Typically Includes

  • Structural and non-structural wall framing
  • Load-bearing wall removal with engineering
  • Custom built-ins, shelving, and millwork
  • Trim, baseboard, and crown moulding
  • Staircase and railing carpentry
