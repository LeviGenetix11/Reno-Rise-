Waterproofing

French Drain Installation: What to Know Before You Hire

A lot of basement moisture problems start above ground, with water pooling near the foundation after every rain because the yard doesn't drain away from the house. A French drain intercepts that water before it becomes a basement problem.

Professionals typically grade and route the drain based on how water actually moves across your property, not a generic straight line to the street.

What This Work Typically Includes

  • Site assessment of water flow patterns
  • Trenching and perforated pipe installation
  • Gravel and filter fabric backfill
  • Discharge point routed away from the foundation
