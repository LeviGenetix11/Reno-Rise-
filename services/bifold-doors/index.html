Bifold Door Installation in the GTA

Home Services Bifold Door Installation

Dollar figures and timelines on this page are illustrative planning ranges from general industry information; they are not quotes, may be out of date, and have not been independently verified by Reno Rise.

Quick answer: bifold door installation typically costs $250 to $1,200 per opening, depending on the door material and hardware. Most standard closet and laundry installs land at the lower end of that range.

Bifold doors give you full access to a closet or laundry nook without the floor clearance a swinging door demands, which is why they show up in almost every tight utility space in GTA homes. They fold flat against the frame instead of swinging into the room.

What Bifold Door Installation Actually Involves

The job starts with checking whether the opening and frame are actually square, since a bifold door hung on an out-of-square opening is the most common reason they stick or sag within a year. From there, they select the right track for the door's weight, hang and align the panels, and fit the pulls and guides so the door folds smoothly without catching.

Pricing depends mostly on door material. A standard hollow-core bifold runs $250-$450 installed. Solid-core or custom-sized doors run $450-$900. Mirrored doors or full closet-system panels run $600-$1,200, mainly due to the hardware and weight-rated track involved.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Installing on an out-of-square opening without shimming the frame first.
  • Using a lightweight track rated for hollow-core doors under a heavier solid or mirrored panel.
  • Skipping final pivot and guide adjustment, which is what actually stops panels from dragging on the floor.

When Bifold Doors Aren't the Right Fit

If the closet is deep and you want full unobstructed access with no centre post in the way, a sliding or pocket door often works better than folding panels. And if the opening in question is a high-traffic entry rather than a closet, a bifold isn't built for that kind of daily use; the standard door installation service is the better fit there.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does bifold door installation cost?

A standard hollow-core bifold door typically costs $250-$450 installed per opening. Solid-core or custom-sized doors run $450-$900. Mirrored or full closet-system doors run $600-$1,200 depending on size and hardware.

Do I need a permit to install bifold doors?

No. Bifold door installation is a finish-level job that doesn't touch structure, plumbing, or electrical, so it doesn't require a permit.

How long does bifold door installation take?

A single opening typically takes 1-2 hours once the frame is confirmed square. If the opening needs to be shimmed or reframed first, plan for closer to half a day.
