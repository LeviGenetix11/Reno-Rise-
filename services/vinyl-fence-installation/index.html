Vinyl Fence Installation: What to Know Before You Hire

Vinyl fence installation trades the annual staining and repainting a wood fence demands for a higher upfront cost and basically zero maintenance after that. For homeowners who want a fence and not a recurring chore, that trade makes sense — as long as the posts are set correctly, since vinyl won't forgive a sloppy footing the way wood sometimes does.

Professionals typically set every post in concrete below the frost line and use manufacturer-specified panel spacing, so the fence line stays straight and the panels don't pop out of their brackets after the first freeze-thaw cycle.

What This Work Typically Includes

  • Maintenance-free vinyl panel and post systems
  • Concrete footings below the frost line
  • Privacy, semi-privacy, and picket-style panels
  • Manufacturer warranty registration (confirm who handles it)
