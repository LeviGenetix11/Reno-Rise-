Accessibility and Aging-in-Place

Accessibility and Aging-in-Place, Done Right the First Time

Most homes weren't built with mobility changes in mind, and retrofitting one after an injury or diagnosis under time pressure usually costs more than planning ahead does.

We design accessibility upgrades to look like part of the home, not an add-on, using fixtures and finishes that work as well as they look.

What's Included

  • Curbless and walk-in shower conversions
  • Grab bar and support rail installation
  • Doorway widening for wheelchair access
  • Stair lift and ramp preparation
  • Lever hardware and accessible fixture upgrades
