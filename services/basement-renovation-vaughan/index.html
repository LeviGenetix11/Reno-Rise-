Vaughan's basements tend to be bigger than the GTA average, and the scale changes what's actually worth building. In Woodbridge and Maple, large production and custom homes from the past two decades usually come with basements already poured to code-height, often 1,500 square feet or more sitting unfinished under a fully built main floor. That much space deserves more than a couch and a TV against the wall.

Out in Kleinburg, Vaughan's older heritage village and the estate properties around it, the story shifts slightly. Lots are larger still, but some of the homes are older, which means the same moisture and ceiling-height check applies before any build-out starts. Once that's confirmed, a Kleinburg basement often has the footprint for something genuinely ambitious: a home theatre, a wine cellar, a full in-law suite.

Whether it's Woodbridge, Maple, Vellore Village, or Kleinburg, we design Vaughan basements as second living levels, not rec rooms with better carpet, with the same finish quality as the floors above.

What a Basement Renovation Costs in Vaughan

Pricing follows the same tiers as anywhere in the GTA, but Vaughan projects often land toward the top of each tier simply because the basements themselves are larger. A basic finished shell runs $25,000-$45,000. A mid-range renovation with a bedroom and full bathroom runs $55,000-$85,000. A full secondary living level with a kitchenette, more common on larger Woodbridge, Maple, and Kleinburg lots, can pass $150,000. When This Isn't the Right Fit

Not every Vaughan basement needs a full renovation, and we'd rather say so than sell more scope than the space calls for.

If your basement is already dry and code-height, which is common in Woodbridge and Maple's newer builds, and you just want a straightforward rec room, a full renovation is more than you need; our lighter-scope basement finishing service covers that instead. If you're in an older Kleinburg-area home and there's visible water staining or a musty smell, that needs to be diagnosed through interior waterproofing before any finishing conversation is worth having. And if you're picturing a full home theatre or in-law suite but the budget doesn't match that scope yet, our design and planning service can sequence the project in phases instead of cutting corners on one big build.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a basement renovation cost in Vaughan?

Most Vaughan basement renovations run $25,000 to $160,000, but many land toward the top of that range given how large Vaughan basements tend to be. A mid-range renovation with a bedroom and full bathroom typically runs $55,000-$85,000; a full secondary living level with a kitchenette can pass $150,000.

Are basements in Woodbridge and Maple usually easier to renovate?

Often, yes. Woodbridge and Maple's larger production and custom builds tend to have basements already poured to code-height with more generous square footage, so the work is usually a finishing job rather than a structural one, just at a bigger scale than average.

What can a Kleinburg-area basement typically become?

On larger Kleinburg estate lots, we see basements built out as home theatres, wine cellars, and full in-law suites, since the footprint usually supports it. Older Kleinburg homes still get the same moisture and ceiling-height check as anywhere else before that build-out starts.

Do I need a permit for a basement renovation in Vaughan?

Yes, if the work includes an egress window, new plumbing, electrical beyond a like-for-like swap, or underpinning. A cosmetic refresh of an already-finished, code-compliant basement typically doesn't need one.
