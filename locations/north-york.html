Renovating North York Homes, One Room at a Time

North York is full of postwar bungalows and 1960s-80s split-levels on generous lots, many now in their second or third generation of owners.

That generation gap shows up fast once you open a wall. Original 60-amp panels, single-layer insulation, and layouts built for a very different kitchen habit than today's are the norm here, not the exception. We plan for it from the first walkthrough.

Willowdale, Bayview Village, and Don Mills all carry that same postwar-to-1980s baseline, with newer high-rise condo growth now filling in around them.

Bathroom renovation and basement waterproofing are the projects with the clearest confirmed demand we see in North York, alongside broader home renovation and general contracting work.

Bathroom renovation demand here tracks closely with the neighbourhood's age: a bungalow or split-level from the 1960s-80s usually still has its original layout, and updating it properly means new plumbing rough-in as much as new tile. We treat those as connected decisions, not separate line items.

Basement waterproofing shows up as its own confirmed category in North York more than in most GTA municipalities, which fits homes of this era: older grading and aging foundation waterproofing that was never built to today's standard. We check the actual source of any moisture before recommending a fix, rather than assuming every wet basement needs the same solution. General contracting and broader home renovation demand round out the picture, which tracks with how many North York projects touch more than one trade at once, electrical, plumbing, and structural work alongside the finishes. We coordinate all of it under one crew rather than handing you between separate contractors mid-project.
