Renovating Brock Homes, One Room at a Time

Brock's rural, agricultural character means larger acreages and farmhouses rather than standard subdivision lots.

Renovating a farmhouse is its own discipline: older framing, additions built in different decades, and utilities that don't always match modern code. We've done enough of them to know what to expect.

Beaverton, Cannington, and Sunderland each add their own small-town core to that rural mix. Kitchen, bathroom, and basement work make up most of what we're asked to do here.

We don't have Brock-specific search data to point to, so we'll say plainly what we do know: farmhouse renovations here tend to take longer to scope properly than a standard subdivision job, and rushing that step is how change orders happen later.
