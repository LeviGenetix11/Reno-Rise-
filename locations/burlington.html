Renovating Burlington Homes, One Room at a Time

Burlington's family suburban homes from the 1970s through 90s, alongside newer developments, make up a steady and varied renovation market.

Whichever decade your home was built in, the same question applies: is the layout working against you, or just looking dated? Usually it's both, and usually the fix costs less than people expect.

Aldershot and Roseland carry much of that older, established character, while newer areas like Millcroft add more recent suburban growth to the mix.

Bathroom renovation shows the clearest confirmed demand we see in Burlington, alongside steady interest in outdoor projects like deck building.

That bathroom demand fits a city where most homes are decades old but genuinely well maintained, original layouts and fixtures rather than deferred repairs. It's usually a want, not a need, which changes the conversation: we spend more time on what the homeowner actually wants the room to do, double vanity, curbless shower, better storage, than on emergency triage. Deck building demand follows a similar pattern on the outdoor side, driven by homeowners adding real usable space rather than fixing something broken. We treat both categories the same way: an in-person walkthrough before a quote, regardless of whether the project is inside the house or in the backyard, and regardless of which established Burlington neighbourhood it's in.
