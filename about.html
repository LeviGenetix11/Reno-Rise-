Who We Are

Built Because GTA Homeowners Were Tired of Guessing.

15k+ Happy Clients Trusted across the region

Reno Rise was built for one reason: homeowners across the Greater Toronto Area were tired of guessing. Tired of cold-calling five contractors and getting five different answers. Tired of not knowing who's actually good until the drywall's already up. We set out to fix that, from Toronto and Scarborough to Ajax, Pickering, and Oakville.

Every professional who works under the Reno Rise name is licensed and insured, and held to one standard: real craftsmanship, real communication, and work you'd be proud to show off. We don't think of a renovation as an expense, it's a correction, restoring the comfort, function, and value your home already deserves. That's why every project starts with an in-person assessment instead of a phone-quote guess, and every quote is itemized so you can see exactly where the money goes.

We're local. We're GTA-born. And we're picky about who we put in front of you, on every trade a project touches.
